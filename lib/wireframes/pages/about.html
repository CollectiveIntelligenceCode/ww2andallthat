About

History you can walk into

WW2 & All That is James Holland’s home on the web: the essays, the films, the documents and the maps, told one campaign at a time, with the people who care about it in the room. It grew out of the books and the podcast, and it is made by a small team in England.

The people

Photo

James Holland

Historian, author of Fortress Malta, The Savage Storm and Brothers in Arms among many others, and co-host of the We Have Ways of Making You Talk podcast. He writes every essay here and presents every film. Writing The Atlantic War.

Photo

Paul Davis

Runs the company and the site: the films, the website, the membership. Previously a creative director and producer in digital agencies. The one who replies when you write to us.

The crew

A small rotating team of camera operators, editors, researchers and designers, credited on every master cut. Plus the historians and curators who appear in the films, named as they appear.

The company

WW2 & All That is published by James Holland WW2 Ltd, registered in England and Wales, 27 Mortimer Street, London W1T 3BL. The YouTube channel is WW2 Headquarters. We are independent: no sponsor decides what we make, and membership pays for the films.
