Which fighter arrived on Malta in March 1942 and changed the air battle over the island?

Not quite. The Gladiators were Malta’s first defenders in 1940 (Faith, Hope and Charity, though there were more than three). The first Spitfires flew in from HMS Eagle on 7 March 1942, and with Park’s Forward Interception Plan that summer they turned the air battle.
