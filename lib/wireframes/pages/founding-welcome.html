From James

Dear Paul,

I have wanted to make something like this for years: history you can walk into, with the films and the documents and the maps in one place, and the people who care about it in the room. A founding membership is a bet on that idea before it has proved itself, and I do not take it lightly.

Every new book I write will come to you signed. Your name sits in the credits of every master cut. Your questions go to the front of the queue, and when we run a tour you hear first. And the first live chat is yours: founding members only, no agenda, bring a question.

James
