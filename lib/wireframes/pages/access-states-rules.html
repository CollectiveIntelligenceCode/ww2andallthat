Access states

What each kind of visitor sees

Three states, set by the Ghost tier the front end looks up on every request: visitor (not logged in), free (logged in, free tier), paid (Member or Founding). The rule is simple: a visitor or free subscriber never reaches a page without a call to action on screen, and never reaches paid content at all. Index and hub pages get a soft prompt and Members tags; content pages get a hard gate where the content stops.

Hard gateBlack-bordered box where content stops. Text or list above it fades out. Primary button: Become a member. Visitors also get Subscribe free. Always a Log in link.
CoverDark overlay on a player or document page that cannot play or be read. Same buttons as the gate.
Soft promptBlue-tinted box on content the viewer can already read. Nudges to the next step up: subscribe free, or become a member.
Members label and sticky barInline label on anything locked (buttons, tabs, tags). Sticky bar at the bottom of every page for visitors and free subscribers, dismissable for the session.
