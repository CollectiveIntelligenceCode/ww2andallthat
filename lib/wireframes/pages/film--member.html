Inside the Lascaris War Rooms

Beneath Valletta, the rooms where the siege of Malta was fought hour by hour, with archive film seen nowhere else.

Friday 26 February 2027·Master cut, 42 min·14 comments

The entrance is easy to miss: a door in the bastion wall below the Upper Barrakka Gardens, and then forty metres of tunnel cut into the limestone. In 1942 this was the most important room on the island, and for most of that year it was the most important room in the Mediterranean.

In this film James walks the plotting room with the museum's curator, follows one day of the siege through the original logs, and uses archive footage from the Pathé library to show what the plots on the table meant in the sky above.

Photograph · 3:2
The plotting table today · Imperial War Museum / placeholder credit
“Every red counter on that table was a raid, and in April 1942 the table was never empty.”

The master cut runs to 42 minutes and includes the full archive sequence from Operation Pedestal; the short cut is the nine-minute version that also runs on YouTube.
