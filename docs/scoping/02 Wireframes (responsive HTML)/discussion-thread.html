Page 31 of the Pedestal orders: whose handwriting is this?

Started by Convoy42 · 4 days ago·18 replies · 23 likes · 7 peopleJames replied
Convoy42Member4 days ago

The amendments on page 31 of the convoy orders look like a different hand from the rest of the handwritten notes. The ink is darker and the 7s are crossed, which none of the other annotations have. Does anyone recognise it, or know who at Gibraltar would have been amending the escort allocation that late?

Crossed 7s were standard for anyone trained in the Continental manner, which narrows it a bit. Burrough’s staff had at least one RNVR officer who had been at school in Switzerland.
