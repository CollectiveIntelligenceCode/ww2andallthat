The question came from MaltaGC in the Ask James room: if Gibraltar guarded the entrance to the Mediterranean, why did Malta matter more? James recorded this in the car outside Kew, which is why there is a lorry at 1:40.

The short answer is geography: Gibraltar guards a door, Malta sits in the middle of the room. The long answer is the one in the full version, and it runs through Rommel’s supply lines, the 10th Submarine Flotilla and why Churchill would not let the island go.

0:00So the question is why Malta and not Gibraltar, and I think the honest answer is that both mattered, but they mattered for different things.

2:18Gibraltar is a door. You hold it, you decide who comes into the Mediterranean from the Atlantic. Malta is a table in the middle of the room, and from it you can reach everything.

3:40Every convoy from Italy to Libya passes within range of Malta. That is the whole thing, really. Rommel’s fuel went past Malta.
