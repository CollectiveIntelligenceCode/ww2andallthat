About WW2 & All That

What is this?

James Holland’s home on the web: the Second World War told one campaign at a time, in essays, films, maps, documents and live conversation, published by James Holland WW2 Ltd.

How is it different from the podcast and the YouTube channel?

The podcast and YouTube are free and public. This is where the full films, the archive and James’s writing live, and where members talk to James and each other.

Where can I read it?

Here, on any device, and in your inbox every Friday.

Membership and access

What do I get free?

Each Friday’s essay, in full, the week it comes out. Every Lessons of History piece. The short cut of every film. The first question of every quiz.

What does membership add?

The full master-cut films with the archive footage, the whole archive, maps and documents, live chats and replays, quizzes and badges, the discussion rooms, James’s blog and the reading list. £5 a month or £50 a year.

What is a founding membership?

£250 a year, for a limited number of people who want to back the first year: everything in membership plus a signed copy of every new book, a personal welcome note, questions answered first, your name in the credits, and first refusal on tours. Includes one gift membership a year.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from Account › Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. UK buyers also have a 14-day right to cancel a new subscription for a refund.

I subscribed on Substack. What happens now?

Your subscription, free or paid, moved across with you. Log in with the same email address. Paid members keep their renewal date and card; payments now appear as James Holland WW2 Ltd on your statement.

Gifts

Can I give membership as a gift?

Yes. From Account › Subscription, choose Send a gift, pick a date, and the recipient gets a welcome email that day.

What is a gift link?

Members can open any members-only post to a friend for one read, twice a month, from the Share menu on the post or from Account › Refer a friend.

Your account

How do I log in?

Enter your email on the sign-in page and we send you a link. There is no password.

How do I change my email, card or plan?

Account › Account for your email; Account › Subscription for your card and plan.

How do I change my leaderboard name, or hide myself?

Account › Badges. Only your chosen name is ever shown.

Emails

Which emails will I get?

The Friday essay, and a short welcome series when you join. Members can also turn on reminders for live chats and replies in Discussions. Everything is managed in Account › Notifications.

The emails are not arriving

Check your spam or promotions folder and add the sender to your contacts. If they still do not arrive, use the form below and we will look at your address.

Still need help?

Send us a message. We reply within two working days.
